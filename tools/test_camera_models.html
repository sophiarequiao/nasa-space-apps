Posture test without a camera

A reference portrait is drawn on a canvas, moved and turned by known amounts, and shown to face.js and body.js as if it were the camera. What they report is compared with what was drawn. It takes about 70 seconds and needs no camera or microphone.

The portrait is the test picture Google publishes with MediaPipe. It is loaded from Google's servers, as are the two models if they are not in vendor/mediapipe; nothing is stored here and nothing leaves this page. Keep this tab in view while it runs: browsers slow down hidden tabs. A still picture checks the geometry (tilt, movement, shifts). It cannot check what a real person's change of posture means.
